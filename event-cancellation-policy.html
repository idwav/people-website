Event Cancellation Policy

Last updated · 11 October 2026

Guest cancellations

Unless an event listing states otherwise, cancellations can be made up to 48 hours before the event. After that point, reservations are non-refundable.

Sold out and transferred places

A sold-out event cannot accept additional reservations. A place may be transferred to another guest only with prior notice and approval from PÉOPLE.

Cancelled or rescheduled events

If PÉOPLE cancels or reschedules an event, registered guests will be contacted directly. Any eligible payment will be refunded or transferred to the new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to people@zipiromanogroup.co.il.

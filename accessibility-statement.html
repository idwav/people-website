Accessibility Statement

Last updated · 11 October 2026

Our commitment

PÉOPLE aims to provide a website that can be used by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

What we support

We work to provide keyboard navigation, readable contrast, meaningful headings, alternative text, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support where relevant.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, tell us which page and feature caused difficulty. We will review the issue and provide an accessible alternative where possible.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to people@zipiromanogroup.co.il.

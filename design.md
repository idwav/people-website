# PÉOPLE × Luminasphere — Design Specification

Reference: https://luminasphere.framer.website/

The PÉOPLE site uses the Luminasphere site as its direct visual and interaction system, while retaining every PÉOPLE section, message, image and call to action.

## Exact visual grammar

- Fixed, transparent three-part header: navigation, centered wordmark, contact/meta edge.
- IBM Plex Mono is the primary English typeface. Assistant is used only for Hebrew.
- Regular and medium weights replace oversized condensed bold typography.
- The opening screen is a full lime colour field with a central dotted P identity mark.
- Content is arranged in large editorial pages: sparse white space, low-density metadata and image-led blocks.
- Project-like sections use paired titles and descriptions, wide media and thin rules.
- Lists use compact numbered mono rows with generous vertical space.
- Dark sections are reserved for cinematic portrait or philosophy content.
- Calls to action use thin outlined rectangles and black inversion on hover.
- Pink, blue, lime, orange and violet appear as precise circular markers. There are no stickers.

## Layout

- Desktop outer gutter: 20px.
- Fixed header height: 42px.
- Section padding: 112–208px depending on density.
- White sections alternate with a lime opening page and selected black editorial pages.
- RTL Hebrew remains right-aligned and readable inside the same modular grid.
- Mobile collapses every multi-column composition to a single editorial column.

## Typography

- English UI, titles and body: IBM Plex Mono 400–500.
- Hebrew: Assistant 400–700.
- Large headings may be large in area but stay regular weight, with close mono tracking.
- Metadata: 10–13px uppercase mono.
- Running text: 14–20px with relaxed line height.

## Motion

- The preloader introduces PÉOPLE, then slides away.
- Section content re-enters when it returns to the viewport.
- Hovering audience rows reveals the related image as a left-side sliding panel.
- Colour dots scale subtly on interactive states.
- Motion remains short, linear and editorial; no sticker pops or oversized bounce.

## Avoid

- Stickers, paper notes and round badges.
- Anton-style condensed billboard headings.
- Thick dividers and dense bordered grids.
- Large blocks of arbitrary accent colour outside the opening identity page.
- Extra fonts, decorative scripts and inconsistent type scales.

## PÉOPLE colour-and-dot language

The supplied moodboard is part of the core identity, not a temporary decoration.

- Use saturated primary and near-primary colours: red, cobalt, cyan, green, yellow, orange, pink and deep violet.
- Circles may sit over photography as flat colour discs, act as cut-outs that reveal image fragments, or punctuate metadata and numbers.
- Image overlays should feel composed like print collage: irregular but intentional, with varied scale and asymmetric placement.
- Colour blocks stay small and geometric so the Luminasphere spacing system remains visible.
- Black-and-white photography plus bright dots is the preferred image treatment.
- Dots replace stickers; never add paper shadows, handwritten labels or tilted note cards.

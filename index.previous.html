Who we are

מי אנחנו

Not a studio. Not a school. A creative community of Péople.

People 4 People

A community for the creators of a new culture — one that honors the free human being’s right to be authentic and unique, exactly as they were born to be. Built on a genuine desire to do good for creation, generating a unified human flow that enables value‑driven growth within a safe space.

Our values are rooted in a love of people, an acceptance of difference, and the understanding that every person can play a meaningful role in another’s life. The goal is a space where every person sheds the old shell, and connects to a new world that celebrates what makes them singular. Only from that place will they feel valued, fulfilled, and able to contribute to themselves and to the community.

We are the — People 4 People.

קהילה יצירתית שנולדה מתוך אמונה שכל אדם נושא בתוכו צבע ייחודי. זהות, כישרון, ואנרגיה שאי אפשר לחקות. הקהילה נועדה לגלות, לפתח ולחגוג את הזהות האמיתית של כל אדם ואת אזור הגאונות שלו.

Our Philosophy

You are the only you.

A philosophy that celebrates the distinction between one person and another. Years of research into the individual — in both the physical and the psychic space — show that no two people are alike, to a striking degree.

Every person has their own area of genius: a calling, a purpose, a craft. PÉOPLE exists to help you identify that inner genius, and to live it out fully.

Founder

ZipiRomanoGroup · 2005 · Tel Aviv

Romano.

Zipi Romano

An entrepreneur, cultural brand architect, and creative strategist. In 2005, she founded a branding, strategy, and public relations studio that has worked with some of the most influential lifestyle, fashion, and beauty brands in Israel and internationally.

Driven by the belief that authentic branding begins from within, she developed a methodology that helps individuals and organizations discover their core identity and translate it into visual language, narrative, and cultural presence.

She is also the founder of PÉOPLE — a creative community and cultural platform connecting creators, entrepreneurs, and thinkers, building new bridges between identity, culture, and opportunity.

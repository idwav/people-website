# PÉOPLE — Community, Fine House and People Studio

This is the active design direction for these three pages. It is derived from the current home page and the finished Academy pages. Earlier page-specific design notes do not govern these pages.

## Source of truth

1. The live home page defines the site grid, navigation, typography and relationship between text, image and whitespace.
2. The Academy landing page and its five product pages define the editorial pacing, section composition and content hierarchy.
3. The approved `Work / Create / Host` section on Fine House is locked. Its composition and colors stay intact.
4. Page copy comes from the approved product specification. Design must clarify it, never replace it with invented copy.

## Core visual language

- White is the primary field. Black is the primary type color.
- Images remain in full color. Grayscale is reserved for event material only when already established there.
- Use Montserrat for large display typography and Albert Sans for labels, body text, navigation and metadata.
- Every section begins from the site gutter: `clamp(20px, 2.1vw, 40px)`.
- Large type is editorial and architectural. It may overlap an image intentionally, but it must remain readable and stay inside the viewport.
- Supporting copy sits on a clear opposing axis. Avoid centering headings and paragraphs together.
- Use asymmetry with balance: one dominant title, one dominant image, one supporting text block. Extra images require a clear narrative job.
- Whitespace must frame a composition. Avoid empty space that remains after the composition has ended.
- Numbering always uses three digits: `001`, `002`, `003`.

## Section grammar

### 1. Cover

- One viewport high on desktop.
- Small index label at the upper gutter.
- One complete page name at a single typographic scale.
- One dominant color image.
- One compact descriptive paragraph on the opposite side.
- One clear text action.
- Optional accent: one small colored dot. No decorative image fragments without a content role.

### 2. Editorial statement

- Use the Academy statement pattern: a large three-part sentence with staggered alignment.
- Pair it with two short paragraphs or one paragraph and one image.
- The composition fills the section; it does not sit as a small centered island.

### 3. Numbered content index

- Use the Academy curriculum pattern for lists, services, formats and benefits.
- Each item has a three-digit number, a direct title and concise supporting copy.
- Prefer an open grid over cards, borders, pills or floating colored words.

### 4. Image-led narrative

- Use one large image and one text block, or one large image with a smaller secondary image only when the second image adds a different piece of information.
- Keep images rectangular and aligned to the grid. No rotation or slanted frames.
- Cropping must preserve faces, hands and the subject of the scene.

### 5. Offer or action

- Calls to action use the Academy registration or next-page rhythm: strong heading on one side, concise action on the other.
- Avoid generic button clusters and decorative labels.

## Page direction

### Community / Creative People

The page should feel populated and human. Use the home page’s community and audience compositions: a strong opening portrait, a clear statement of who the community is for, a numbered index of community value, and selected people or moments as editorial evidence. Avoid a conventional member-card directory.

### Fine House 4 People

The page should feel spatial and usable. The hero name is `FINE HOUSE 4 PEOPLE`, with every word at the same typographic level. Show the house through large color photography, a concise spatial statement, the locked `Work / Create / Host` spread, and a numbered program index. Avoid room cards and unrelated detail images.

### People Studio

The page should feel like active production. Build it from the Academy’s method and process sections: a decisive studio cover, an editorial statement about ideas becoming real, a numbered process or capability index, and one image-led culture/execution story. Avoid colored word clouds, generic service cards and filler galleries.

## Locked Work / Create / Host spread

- The title is one line: `WORK.CREATE.HOST.`
- The full composition is vertically balanced in one desktop viewport.
- Three equal image frames span the full site width between gutters.
- Footer colors match the home page exactly:
  - Work: `#004fff`
  - Create: `#e83a95`
  - Host: `#00c112`
- Each footer includes its original home-page descriptor and a fully visible title.
- The section must not leave a large unused white field below the cards.

## Do not use

- Centered title-plus-paragraph stacks as the default composition.
- Random small images at page edges.
- Colored word lists or color used as decoration without meaning.
- Generic equal cards for editorial content.
- Borders, strokes or dividers that are absent from the reference section.
- Slanted images, rotated frames or presentation-template effects.
- Invented copy, placeholder sections or duplicated statements.
- Oversized type that collides with navigation, supporting copy or viewport edges.

## Desktop quality check

- The section reads as one composition at first glance.
- Text does not collide, clip or overflow.
- The visual center of mass is balanced vertically and horizontally.
- All key content is visible in the intended viewport.
- Empty space has a clear compositional purpose.
- Images are full color and correctly cropped.

## Mobile quality check

- Preserve hierarchy rather than desktop positioning.
- Stack content in reading order: label, heading, copy, image, action.
- Keep three-digit numbering.
- Avoid horizontal clipping and forced single-line display titles.
- Let sections grow naturally; do not force desktop viewport heights on mobile.
